Answers

Frequently asked questions

Is my business data private and isolated?

Yes. Each business runs on its own isolated workspace. Your sales, stock, customers, and finances are fully separated from every other business on the platform — no one else can see your data, and you can’t see theirs.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No long lock-in. We start with a demo and onboard you hands-on. Plans are month-to-month, and we’ll walk you through exactly what’s included before you commit.

What happens when the internet goes down?

Your POS keeps working. Sales are recorded locally and sync automatically the moment your connection is back — so you never miss a transaction during a brownout or a dropped line.

Are the receipts and reports BIR-compliant?

VendoPOS is built peso-first with BIR-ready receipts and reports, so tax time doesn’t mean rebuilding everything by hand. We’ll confirm the specifics for your business type during onboarding.

Can my Customers pay with GCash and Maya?

Yes — GCash, Maya, and QRPH alongside cash, all rung up the same simple way at the till.

How do I get started?

Request a demo below. There’s no self-serve signup — our team onboards you personally, sets up your workspace, and trains your staff so you’re running from day one.
